Capabilities

Your company's operational command center.

Katana HR

People operations

Run hiring, onboarding, and employee records without a separate HRIS. Track goals, reviews, and org changes as your team grows—every role and skill feeds directly into project staffing and workforce scheduling. When people data lives in the same OS as delivery and operations, HR stops being a silo.

Katana PM

Project management

Plan projects, milestones, and tasks in one workspace with clear owners and live status. See dependencies, blockers, and progress without chasing updates across email and spreadsheets. Because PM shares data with CRM, inventory, and HR, schedules and commitments stay tied to real capacity and customer demand.

Katana Customers

Customer relationships

Capture leads, qualify opportunities, and move deals through your pipeline with full context at every stage. Every call, note, and agreement sits on the account record from first touch through renewal. Sales, delivery, support, and billing stay connected—so handoffs don't lose history when work moves downstream.

Katana Inventory

Inventory control

Know quantities by location, SKU, and bin with live adjustments as goods move. Scan items in, pick for jobs, and transfer stock with a clear audit trail tied to projects and orders. Purchase orders surface from real demand—so you're not reconciling inventory in one tool and fulfillment in another.

Katana Workforce

Workforce scheduling

Build schedules around skills, sites, and availability—with coverage you can see before shifts go live. Track hours against jobs, projects, and cost centers so labor rolls up to how the business actually runs. Workforce stays aligned with HR records and PM load, so staffing reflects who's hired and what work is due.

Know Your Investor

Investor relations

Keep investor profiles, ownership, and cap table context in one registry instead of scattered decks and spreadsheets. Log meetings, updates, and documents on each relationship so comms history is searchable when diligence starts. Export board-ready summaries when you need them—fundraising stays organized and auditable across every touchpoint.
