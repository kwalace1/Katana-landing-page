Talk to us

VEYAH is Katana’s AI-powered data translation platform. It does not dump columns from one tool into another. It reads what the data means, reshapes it for the destination, and delivers it with the links between records still intact.

Use it to bring existing systems into Katana, or to move data between other platforms — Salesforce, HubSpot, Jira, Zendesk, spreadsheets, APIs, and custom schemas.

The problem it solves

Business software was never designed to speak the same language. A “customer” in one system is a “client” in another. Statuses, dates, phone numbers, and IDs rarely line up. Spreadsheet imports break. Relationships get lost. Migrations stall in mapping hell.

VEYAH is built for that gap: understand first, then move.

How it works

Understand → Translate → Validate → Deliver

01

Bring the data in

Upload CSV or Excel, connect a source system, or send records through an API. Files stay quarantined until you release them for processing.

02

Understand

VEYAH detects the shape of the file: columns, entity types, business domain, and how records relate — contacts to accounts, tasks to people, tickets to organizations.

03

Translate

It proposes field mappings with business context, not just matching column names. Formats get cleaned. You review and edit every proposal before anything is sent.

04

Validate and deliver

A preview shows what will land, including issues and recommended fixes. When you approve, VEYAH pushes to the destination with delivery logs, safe reruns, and rollback.

In the product

Chat is the mapping. Certified means ready to send.

Bring a file or a connected source. Pick where it goes. Review the match. Send when it’s certified.

What makes it different from a typical importer

  • Meaning over names. The same word can mean different things in two systems. VEYAH maps intent, not labels.
  • Relationships stay attached. Contacts belong to accounts. Tasks belong to people. Those links travel with the records.
  • You stay in control. Nothing is delivered until you preview and approve. AI proposals can always be inspected and changed.
  • Built for real cutovers. Initial loads, incremental updates, and a final coordinated cutover — not a one-shot dump.
  • Safe to operate. Encrypted connectors, organization isolation, retention policies, audit trails, and the ability to undo a delivery.

Where it fits in Katana

Katana Business runs the company: projects, customers, people, inventory, field work, hiring, investors, finance, and more.

VEYAH is how data gets there — and how it moves between other tools when Katana is not the destination. When someone imports into a Katana module, VEYAH is the translation layer: detect, map, preview, then push clean records into the right Katana entities.

It is not a Katana-only importer. It is a neutral translation layer that also happens to be the native on-ramp into Katana.

Who it is for

Teams leaving another platform, consolidating tools, or feeding Katana from spreadsheets and APIs — without a six-month mapping project.

Typical moves: CRM to CRM, support tickets into project tracking, HR and inventory files into Katana, or any structured export into a defined destination schema.
