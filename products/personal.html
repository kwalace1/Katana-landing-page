    How time is calculated · sources

    Method. Estimated scattered time = category activity averages (counted once per life area you cover) + a multi-app switch model of 5 minutes per extra app, capped at 36 minutes/day. Free DIY options use 25% more activity time. Katana is modeled as one app (~10–12 min/day), so the switch add-on is zero.

    What the sources are — and are not. Linked studies report findings in their contexts (for example workplace app toggling, food-diary logging, ChatGPT visit length). We adapted those published averages into this calculator. They do not study Katana, do not endorse Katana, and do not prove that you will save a specific number of hours. Results here are illustrative marketing estimates — not a clinical study, audit, or guarantee.

      Money figures are public US list prices (annual effective rate when that’s the common plan). Free DIY options are $0. Time figures are Katana’s illustrative model citing the sources above — not proven personal savings and not endorsed by those publishers. Katana Personal Free is $0 for the full private day loop.
