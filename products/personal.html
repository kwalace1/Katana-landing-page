The loop

Most life apps become another list.

Katana Personal is built around a simple loop.

When you need more room, Plan and Life are there: tasks, goals, habits, notes, and files; fitness, wellness, and a journal. Those modules feed the day. They do not replace it.

How it works

You can open a space with just a name. Everything lives on this device first — no account required to start.

From Today, Katana picks a next step from what is actually on your plate: an overdue task, something due soon, a habit still open. You capture in plain language (“Call Mom Friday 3pm”), confirm, and keep moving. In the evening you close the day. Once a week you get an honest review of what you actually did.

Ask sits inside that loop. Free covers the day and the chips; Plus is the Accountability pack when you want a deeper coach, Circle challenges, and meal & label AI.

If you connect cloud later, Together turns on. Circles are group streak boards — not a plans inbox. Plans is where friends send tasks and goals. A check-in is optional: only the win card you choose goes to friends or a Circle.

You can add Katana to your phone’s home screen and use it like an app. Save a copy of your space anytime so a cleared browser does not take the week with it.

Who it is for

Busy people who already have too many trackers. Anyone who wants a calmer day, not a fuller dashboard. Friends or a small group who want to stay accountable without turning life into a performance.

It is not a wiki. It is not an endless to-do list. Water, sleep, and workouts are signals inside the day — not the reason the product exists.
