Capabilities

Your company's operational command center.

Katana HR

People operations

Run hiring, onboarding, and employee records without a separate HRIS. Track goals, reviews, and org changes as your team grows — every role and skill feeds directly into project staffing and workforce scheduling.

Katana PM

Project management

Plan projects, milestones, and tasks in one workspace with clear owners and live status. Because PM shares data with CRM, inventory, and HR, schedules stay tied to real capacity and customer demand.

Katana Customers

Customer relationships

Capture leads, qualify opportunities, and move deals through your pipeline with full context. Sales, delivery, support, and billing stay connected — so handoffs don't lose history.

Katana Inventory

Inventory control

Know quantities by location, SKU, and bin with live adjustments as goods move. Purchase orders surface from real demand — so you're not reconciling inventory in one tool and fulfillment in another.

Katana Workforce

Workforce scheduling

Build schedules around skills, sites, and availability. Workforce stays aligned with HR records and PM load, so staffing reflects who's hired and what work is due.

Know Your Investor

Investor relations

Keep investor profiles, ownership, and cap table context in one registry. Log meetings, updates, and documents so fundraising stays organized and auditable.
